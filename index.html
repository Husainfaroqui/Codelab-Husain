What's included — and what isn't

Package prices cover website development. The following are not automatically included and may be charged separately where required:

  • Domain & hosting
  • Paid plugins & paid APIs
  • Premium third-party services
  • Premium assets & external subscriptions

A few things worth knowing up front:

  • Each package has a defined scope and a limited number of revisions
  • Delivery timelines are estimates, based on content/assets arriving on time
  • Additional pages or features can be added for an extra cost
